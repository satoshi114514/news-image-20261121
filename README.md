Six stories from Saturday 21 November 2026, set out as a newspaper front page: Russian strikes on Ukraine, missiles landing in Poland and NATO's answer, a sharp sell-off in AI shares, the International Court of Justice ruling on Myanmar, what followed the Shenzhen summit, and a Peru earthquake beside a medical breakthrough. Made for the make.fun bounty "Sum up 21 November's world news in one image" ($100 in USDC). The masthead carries the date, and each panel sets its headline next to a photograph and a short caption.

## Running it

Run `node main.ts` from the repository root with Node 22 installed.

The program places a copy of entry.png at /output/entry.png. The picture is a 1024-pixel square under one megabyte, and two runs in a row produce identical bytes.
